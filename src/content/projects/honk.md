H.O.N.K. is a game studio I co-founded with three friends. We started it after 8-Bit Jam, where our entry **Space Pirates** won Best Innovation. The prize was six months of Codecrafters.

Four of us: three developers and an artist. I work as a developer. The studio is still active.

## Space Pirates

Built in Godot with GDScript during 8-Bit Jam on 22 August 2026, a single-day jam running from 8:15 AM to 6:00 PM. It won Best Innovation.
